iB COINS + PAYPAL

A simple way to turn belief into action.

iB Coins are the participation units used inside inBestmen. In the planned experience, one iB Coin tracks one US dollar and is purchased securely through PayPal before it is donated to a project.

01Buy with PayPal

Choose an amount and complete checkout through PayPal.

02Receive iB Coins

iB Coins are a platform participation model in this proposal, not a cryptocurrency or promise of financial return. Final payment and legal terms will be published before launch.
